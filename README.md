# PayWeb_CSCart

## Paygate CS-Cart plugin v1.1.0 for CS-Cart 4.18.2

This is the Paygate plugin for CS-Cart. Please feel free to contact the Payfast support team at support@payfast.help
should you require any assistance.

## Installation

1. **Download the Plugin**

    - Visit the [releases page](https://github.com/Paygate/PayWeb_CSCart/releases) and
      download [paygate.zip](https://github.com/Paygate/PayWeb_CSCart/releases/download/v1.1.0/paygate.zip).

2. **Install the Plugin**

    - Log in to your CS-Cart Admin panel.
    - Navigate to **Add-ons > Manage add-ons**.
    - Click the **+** button on the top right.
    - Click **Local** and select `paygate.zip`.
    - Click the **Upload & install** button.
    - Confirm that the **Paygate Payment Gateway** addon is active.

3. **Configure the Plugin**

    - Navigate to **Administration > Payment Methods**.
    - Click the **+** button on the top right.
    - Type **Paygate** as the name.
    - Select **Paygate (Web)** as Processor.
    - Select **Credit Card** as Category.
    - Click the **Configure** tab (next to **General**) and enter the required data.
    - Click the **Create** button.
    - Clear your CS-Cart cache by accessing this URL: http://yourstore/admin.php?cc - Replace **yourstore** with your URL
      and replace **admin.php** with your admin script filename.
